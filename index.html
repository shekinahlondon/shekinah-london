How we look after your information

Your details are held by Shekinah Nation (registered charity 1206699) and are only seen by our church admin team. Prayer requests are only seen by our pastoral team.

We use them to look after you as part of the church, record attendance at our services and act on the choices you've made, such as celebrating your birthday or sending you news. We'll only send you news and updates if you've ticked that box.

Because these details relate to church life, we treat them as sensitive. We never sell them or share them outside the church without your permission.

This form is for adults. Parents or guardians can speak to our Children's team about registering children.

You can ask us to update or delete your details at any time by emailing shekinahnationlondon@gmail.com or speaking to one of our team.
